# TSEM Benchmark Comparison

Research-only comparison context. COHR, LITE, ONTO, CAMT, FORM, AAOI and AXTI are not underwritten here.

## COHR / LITE

COHR and LITE are photonics incumbents with direct optical exposure and broader product/customer cycles. TSEM is more Serenity-style if the question is a narrow foundry/capacity constraint for SiPho manufacturing rather than component cyclicality. However, COHR/LITE may have clearer product revenue visibility in some periods, while TSEM has contract/capacity term opacity.

## ONTO / CAMT / FORM

ONTO, CAMT and FORM are process-control/test/inspection chokepoints. They benefit from complexity and yield burden, but their thesis revenue is often not cleanly separated. TSEM now has cleaner headline contract evidence than these names, but also more contract-specific and capacity-specific risk.

## AAOI / AXTI

AAOI and AXTI are useful false-positive/historical-cycle references: optical/module/substrate narratives can rerate sharply while customer concentration, pricing, China/geopolitics, or cycle risk damages shareholder capture. TSEM's evidence is cleaner than AAOI/AXTI-style narrative screens because it includes filed contracts and prepayments, but the same caution applies: a great supply-chain lane is not automatically clean common-shareholder capture.

## Bottom Line

TSEM is more Serenity-style than the benchmarks because the chokepoint is narrow, physical, customer-funded and time-linked to 2027 SiPho capacity. Its risk is also more concentrated: contract terms, capacity ramp, customer concentration, litigation/IP, and crowding.
