## TSEM

### Refresh Decision

Retain `R7_RISK_FLAG_WATCHLIST`.

### Evidence Improved Or Reconfirmed

- Q1 2026 revenue was $414M, up 15% year over year.
- Q1 2026 operating cash flow included $290M of SiPho customer prepayments.

| prepayment is not revenue | Still open; $290M customer prepayments are capacity reservation and customer advances, not recognized revenue. |
| customer identity / concentration | Still open; largest SiPho customers are not named. |
| capacity execution | Still open; 2025 20-F and Q1 release include capacity/ramp and operational bottleneck risks. |
| GF litigation | Still open; prior filings mention GlobalFoundries patent infringement claims and Tower's dispute. |
| valuation / crowding | Still open; public contract headline is now visible, so execution risk is more important. |

### Confirmation Path

- Recognized SiPho revenue bridge appears in future filings.
- Customer advances/deferred revenue roll-forward shows clean conversion.
- Contract quality, refund mechanics, and additional prepayment schedule become clearer.
- Capacity/yield/customer qualification milestones are publicly confirmed.
- GF litigation resolves or is shown immaterial.

### Invalidation Path

- Prepayment refund, delay, or missed additional prepayment.
- Contract reduction, cancellation, or weak refund protection.
- Capacity ramp or qualification failure.
- Material litigation impairment.
- SiPho revenue materially lags commitment path.

### Confirmation Path

- Customer concentration stabilizes or declines.
- HBM/probe-card cadence continues across multiple quarters.
- Margin and OCF remain supportive.
- CPO/SiPh test contribution becomes more explicit and material.

### Invalidation Path

- Major customer/program weakens.
- HBM/test growth proves cyclical pull-forward.
- Gross margin or OCF deteriorates despite revenue growth.
- Customer concentration rises without durability proof.
