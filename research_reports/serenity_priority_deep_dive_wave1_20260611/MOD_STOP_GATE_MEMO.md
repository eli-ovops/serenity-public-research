## Bottleneck Thesis

MOD maps to the data-center thermal infrastructure chokepoint: chiller capacity, liquid-cooling-adjacent systems, heat rejection, air handling, and industrial delivery schedules for AI factories. The bottleneck is not "cooling theme" in general; it is scarce qualified manufacturing capacity for mission-critical data-center cooling systems.

## Confirmed Evidence

- Modine announced a long-term capacity agreement under which it will guarantee capacity to supply more than $4B of Airedale data-center cooling products during calendar 2027-2029.
- The same announcement says Modine received a $165M upfront cash payment from the customer to support capacity investments and related commitments.
- Q4 FY2026 net sales were $954.4M, up 47% year over year.
- FY2026 net sales were $3.181B, up 23% year over year.
- FY2026 annual-report content says Data Centers represented 35% of FY2026 sales versus 25% in FY2025.
- FY2026 annual-report content says top ten customers accounted for 49% of net sales and one global technology customer in Climate Solutions represented about 11% of total sales.
- The annual-report content also flags component shortages affecting data-center product production schedules for Q1 FY2027.

## Main Unverified Assumptions

- The $4B capacity agreement converts to recognized revenue at the expected cadence.
- The anonymous strategic customer does not defer, reduce, or terminate orders.
- Gross margin recovers as temporary expansion costs, tariffs, material costs, and component shortages normalize.
- Capacity expansion is delivered without significant quality, schedule, or working-capital stress.

## Confirmation Path

- LTA/backlog converts into recognized Data Centers segment revenue.
- Gross margin and operating cash flow improve as capacity expansion scales.
- Component shortages ease and production schedules normalize.
- Data Centers segment reporting after April 2026 split gives clearer profitability and customer evidence.

## Invalidation Path

- The strategic customer defers, reduces, or terminates expected orders.
- Capacity expansion costs keep gross margin structurally lower.
- Component shortages continue to constrain deliveries.
- Working capital absorbs the economics of growth.

## Governance Decision

`MOD` remains `R6_PRIORITY_MEMO_CONTINUE`. It is now one of the strongest AI factory infrastructure candidates, but the customer concentration, order-conversion, capacity, and margin gates prevent R7 upgrade.
