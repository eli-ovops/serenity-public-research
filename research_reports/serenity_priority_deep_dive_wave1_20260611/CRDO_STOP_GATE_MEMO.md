## Bottleneck Thesis

CRDO sits in AI networking connectivity: AEC, SerDes, DSP, retimer, and optical-interconnect adjacency. The Serenity chokepoint is not generic "AI growth"; it is the reliability, power, signal-integrity, and time-to-stability layer between accelerators, switches, and scale-out/scale-up clusters.

## Confirmed Evidence

- Company IR reported Q4 FY2026 revenue of $437.0M, up 157.0% year over year, GAAP gross margin of 68.2%, non-GAAP gross margin of 68.3%, and cash / short-term investments of $1.4B.
- FY2026 revenue more than tripled to $1.3B, and non-GAAP net income increased more than five times to $662M.
- Q1 FY2027 guidance is revenue of $465M-$475M with GAAP gross margin of 66.9%-68.9%.
- Latest available SEC 10-Q language says CRDO sells to hyperscalers, OEMs, ODMs, optical module makers, enterprise, and HPC customers; it also says a relatively small number of customers historically account for a significant portion of revenue.
- The 10-Q describes connectivity solutions for 100G, 200G, 400G, 800G and emerging 1.6T markets, based on SerDes and DSP technologies, including ICs, AECs and SerDes chiplets.

## Main Unverified Assumptions

- The FY2026 ramp is durable across multiple end customers, not mainly one or two hyperscale programs.
- Optical / SiPh / CPO optionality becomes revenue-backed rather than narrative-backed.
- DustPhotonics / acquired or adjacent technology adds durable product breadth without margin degradation.
- Gross margin remains high as revenue scales and product mix changes.

## Confirmation Path

- FY2026 10-K customer concentration and product revenue detail are consistent with broadening customer base.
- Q1 FY2027 revenue lands within or above guide while gross margin remains near guided range.
- Filings or IR materials show more explicit product split across AEC, DSP, retimer, optical, and SiPh/CPO exposure.
- Customer concentration declines or becomes governable through repeat multi-program demand.

## Invalidation Path

- Customer concentration worsens or one top customer drives most incremental revenue.
- Gross margin compresses materially as the ramp scales.
- Optical / SiPh / CPO optionality remains non-material after repeated updates.
- Revenue ramp decelerates before customer diversification is established.

## Governance Decision

`CRDO` remains `R6_PRIORITY_MEMO_CONTINUE`. It is stronger than before, but still needs FY2026 10-K-level customer concentration, margin durability, and product-line linkage review before any R7 vote.
