## Final High-Score Research List

### Tier 1 - Highest Priority

1. CRDO - Credo Technology
   - Role: AI networking / AEC / retimer / optical DSP / SiPh option.
   - Why it scores high: GTC PRIME candidate; current operating evidence is strong; official FY2026 release showed large revenue growth and high gross margin; DustPhotonics adds SiPh/CPO optionality.
   - Main risk: customer concentration, optical revenue split, DustPhotonics integration, margin durability.
   - Verdict: best combined short-term research candidate and still strong for 2-year tracking.

2. FORM - FormFactor
   - Role: HBM / probe card / semiconductor test-interface chokepoint.
   - Why it scores high: Serenity R7 risk-flag watchlist; evidence is filing-backed, operating, and linked to HBM/test complexity.
   - Main risk: SK hynix / NVIDIA customer concentration, memory-cycle sensitivity, exact HBM/CPO/SiPh thesis revenue not separately disclosed.
   - Verdict: highest-quality Serenity candidate; less explosive than TSEM but cleaner evidence.

3. TSEM - Tower Semiconductor
   - Role: SiPho foundry/process-capacity chokepoint.
   - Why it scores high: strongest asymmetric SiPho right-tail in Serenity; official 6-K confirms 2027 SiPho contracts and customer prepayments.
   - Main risk: prepayment is not revenue, non-cancellable/take-or-pay terms not public, anonymous SiPho customers, capacity/yield execution, GF litigation.
   - Verdict: best long-term right-tail candidate, but not clean; must remain R7_RISK_FLAG_WATCHLIST.
