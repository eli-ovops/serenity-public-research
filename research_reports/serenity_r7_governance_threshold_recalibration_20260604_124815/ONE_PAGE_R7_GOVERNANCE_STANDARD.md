# ONE PAGE R7 GOVERNANCE STANDARD

Project: Serenity Alpha Research
Standard: R7 Governance Threshold v1.0

Mandatory statement: None of these categories is a buy recommendation.

## Purpose

R7 is a research-governance watchlist status. It exists to track asymmetric research setups where evidence is real, risks are named, and confirmation/invalidation paths are explicit. R7 is not a purchase decision, target price, position size, entry point, trade plan, conviction portfolio, or current buy list.

## Categories

- R7_CLEAN_WATCHLIST: Evidence strong, no unresolved critical risk flags.
- R7_RISK_FLAG_WATCHLIST: Evidence strong enough for watchlist, but material risks remain and must be monitored.
- R7_SPECULATIVE_RIGHT_TAIL: Right-tail setup exists, evidence incomplete, but not demo-only, hype-only, or revenue-zero without credible path; explicitly speculative.
- R6_PRIORITY_MEMO_CONTINUE: Research quality high, but not ready for R7 even with risk flags.
- R6_BACKLOG: Evidence incomplete; wait for future filings/events/customer disclosure.
- BLOCKED: Critical blocker prevents advancement, but future evidence may reopen.
- NO-GO: Permanent or near-permanent caution under current framework.

## P0 Hard STOP

Block R7 if any of these are active:

- going concern or acute liquidity stress;
- governance/accounting/audit/internal-control impairment;
- weak thesis linkage;
- demo/platform/MoU thesis without production revenue or credible material revenue path;
- social-hype-driven thesis;
- no material operating revenue path tied to the thesis;
- severe dilution/financing that overwhelms survival or common-shareholder capture;
- sanction-linked or uncontrollable geo/export risk that severs the thesis.

## P1 Watchlist Risk Flag

Can enter R7_RISK_FLAG_WATCHLIST if evidence is real and risk is governed:

- customer concentration or customer opacity;
- prepayment not revenue;
- contract cancellability unknown;
- capacity execution unproven;
- dilution/capital-structure complexity not severe enough for P0;
- China/geo/export exposure that is material but monitorable;
- valuation/crowding risk;
- shareholder-capture uncertainty.

## Required R7 Package

Each R7 item must include:

- thesis statement;
- evidence summary;
- risk flags;
- confirmation checklist;
- invalidation checklist;
- review cadence;
- downgrade triggers;
- upgrade triggers;
- source refresh rule;
- crowding / valuation risk review;
- shareholder capture review.

## Review Cadence

- Quarterly filing refresh.
- Event-driven refresh for customer loss, contract amendment, prepayment/refund, capacity milestone, litigation, export-control change, financing, accounting/governance issue, or major capital allocation event.
- Do not create scheduler, cron, service, or monitor.
