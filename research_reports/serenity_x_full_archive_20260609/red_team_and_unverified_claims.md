# RED TEAM AND UNVERIFIED CLAIMS

## High-Level Cautions

- Official full-history access was incomplete, so this cannot be called a complete official archive.
- The main corpus is a public secondary archive. Treat it as a useful index, not final evidence.
- Public mirror pages can be useful for spot checks but are not reliable as complete historical sources.
- Social-media attention is crowding context, not positive evidence.
- Self-reported performance claims are not independently verified here.
- Repeated ticker mentions can reflect attention, controversy, or narrative clustering, not business validation.

## Common Unverified Claim Types

- Supplier identity claims.
- Customer identity claims.
- Capacity scarcity claims.
- Volume-ramp timing claims.
- Forward revenue or margin implications.
- Institutional-flow or holder-change interpretations.
- Claims based on screenshots, images, or reposted snippets.
