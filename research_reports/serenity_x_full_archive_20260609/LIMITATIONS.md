# LIMITATIONS

Decision: PARTIAL.

## Completeness Limits

- The official X full-history stream was not fully accessible in this run through public unauthenticated methods.
- The main usable corpus is a public secondary archive, not an official export.
- Public mirrors and snippets are incomplete and may include relative timestamps, page gaps, ranking bias, duplicate appearances, or stale cache state.
- The indexed corpus covers 5857 unique rows from 2025-07-02T10:48:09+00:00 to 2026-06-08T08:43:24+00:00; content outside that window is not covered unless it appears in the source corpus.
- Long-form article bodies are not reproduced.

## Evidence Limits

- Post attribution is not the same as claim validation.
- Ticker counts are mention counts, not quality scores.
- Theme and chokepoint tags are keyword-derived and require human review.
- Supplier, customer, capacity, ramp, revenue, and outcome claims require separate primary-source checks.

## Project Limits

- This package is external input only.
- Missing root Serenity governance files prevented a current local stop-gate scorecard run.
- No scheduler or persistent process was created.
