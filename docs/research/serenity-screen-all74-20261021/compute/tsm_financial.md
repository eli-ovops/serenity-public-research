**Summary of Cash Flow:**

During the third quarter, net cash generated from operating activities totaled NT$919.77 billion, including (1) NT$911.04 billion from income before tax, (2) NT$220.20 billion from depreciation & amortization expenses, and (3) NT$211.47 billion for other operating uses. The other operating uses were mainly attributable to the income tax payment of NT$197.55 billion.

Net cash used in investing activities was NT$266.79 billion in 3Q26, primarily attributable to capital expenditures of NT$443.21 billion, partially offset by other investing sources of NT$198.74 billion. The other investing sources were mainly attributable to U.S. Treasury Department Investment Tax Credits.

Net cash used in financing activities was NT$160.31 billion, mainly reflecting the payment of 4Q25 cash dividends of NT$155.59 billion.

As a result, cash position increased by NT$487.67 billion to NT$3,621.89 billion at the end of 3Q26.
