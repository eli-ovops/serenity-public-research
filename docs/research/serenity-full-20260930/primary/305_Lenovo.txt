NOTICE OF ADJUSTMENT OF CONVERSION PRICE OF 
US$2,000 MILLION ZERO COUPON 
 CONVERTIBLE BONDS DUE 2033 
Reference is made to the announcements of Lenovo Group Limited (the “Company”) dated 
June 17, 2026, June 18, 2026 and  June 25, 2026 in relation to the Company’s issuance of 
US$2,000 million zero coupon convertible bonds due 2033 (the “Bonds”) (collectively, the 
“Announcements”), and the announcement of the Company dated July 23, 202 6 regarding, 
among other things, the approval by the Shareholders of a cash dividend of HK33.7 cents per 
Share (the “Final Dividend”). Unless the context requires otherwise, capitalized terms used 
herein shall have the same meanings as those defined in the Announcements.  
Pursuant to the Terms and Conditions, the Conversion Price would be adjusted following the 
declaration of the Final Dividend. The initial Conversion Price of HK$ 36.70 per Share will 
be adjusted to HK$ 35.74 per Share with effect from  August 8, 2026, being the date 
immediately following the record date for Shareholders entitled to the Final Dividend.  
All other terms of the Bonds remain unchanged.
