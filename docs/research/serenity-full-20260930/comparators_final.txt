## FY2026

Tokyo,August 4, 2026 -- Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd. (MHI) today announced its consolidated financial results for the first three months of FY2026 ended June 30, 2026.

### Q1 FY2026 Financial Results

Order Intake  | 2,022.4 billion yen (+413.2 billion yen year-on-year)
Revenue  | 1,194.2 billion yen (+160.0 billion yen year-on-year)
Profit from Business Activities  | 159.6 billion yen (+62.9 billion yen year-on-year)
Profit Attributable to Owners of Parent  | 134.6 billion yen (+66.4 billion yen year-on-year)

### FY2026 Earnings Forecast

Order Intake  | 7,000.0 billion yen (vs. previous: -653.6 billion yen)
Revenue  | 5,400.0 billion yen (vs. previous: +425.8 billion yen)
Profit from Business Activities  | 540.0 billion yen (vs. previous: +107.7 billion yen)
Profit Attributable to Owners of Parent  | 380.0 billion yen (vs. previous: +47.8 billion yen)
This estimate is based on a currency exchange rate assumption of US$ 1.00 = ¥150 and €1.00 = ¥180 for the portion yet to be fixed.
