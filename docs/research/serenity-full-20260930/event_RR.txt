Richtech Robotics to Showcase Integrated Industrial Robotics Ecosystem at IMTS 2026

September 10, 2026

LAS VEGAS, Sept. 10, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Richtech Robotics Inc. (Nasdaq: RR) (“Richtech Robotics” or “the Company”), a Nevada-based provider of AI-driven service robots, announces its participation at IMTS 2026, taking place September 14-19 in Chicago, Illinois.
