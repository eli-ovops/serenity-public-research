Investors - Camtek Search for: Investors Camtek is a leading developer and manufacturer of high-end inspection and metrology equipment for the semiconductor industry. Camtek’s systems inspect and measure wafers throughout the production process of semiconductor devices, covering the front and mid-end, and up to the beginning of assembly (Post Dicing Inspection and Metrology). Camtek’s systems inspect wafers for the most demanding semiconductor market segments, including Advanced Interconnect Packaging, Memory, CMOS Image Sensors, MEMS and RF, serving the industry’s leading global IDMs, OSATs and foundries. Camtek’s world-class sales and customer support infrastructure is organized around eight subsidiaries based in the US, Europe, Japan, China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Korea and Singapore.
