Introducing the Nebius AI Builder Program: build AI systems you own on an open, independent stack (https://nebius.com/blog/posts/introducing-the-nebius-ai-builder-program)
citeturn216search6 [wordlim: 200] Published: 2 weeks ago; Crawled: yesterday; September 10, 2026

Introducing the Nebius AI Builder Program: build AI systems you own on an open, independent stack

September 10, 2026

9 mins to read

Today, we’re launching the Nebius AI Builder Program. It gives builders what they need to build and scale across the open AI ecosystem: credits across the stack, cookbooks, education and certifications, expert office hours, and a community of AI builders.

AI is no longer just a model you call. It’s a system you build. And the builders, not a few closed labs, will decide what it becomes. That system is a set of primitives working together: open models and fast inference at the foundation, with orchestration, retrieval, observability, evaluation, and post-training on top. Builders shouldn’t have to inherit all of those decisions from a handful of labs.

Much of the progress in AI is happening in the open now. AI is built by many teams, tested by the community, and improved by anyone with a better idea. That gives builders the choice to run the latest open models, pick the tools that fit, and change their mind later without rebuilding around one vendor.

citeturn216search7 [wordlim: 200] Published: last week; Crawled: yesterday; September 22, 2026

Introducing spot pricing for preemptible VMs

Preemptible VMs now come with dynamic spot pricing: more flexible access to capacity, better control of costs.

September 22, 2026

9 mins to read

A virtualized compute environment creates opportunities for both cloud providers and their customers. One is preemptible VMs (PVMs): interruptible compute for non-critical workloads that runs on pre-allocated, temporarily idle capacity. For the GPU compute market, which is still dealing with a scarcity problem, this distribution model presents an elegant solution. Today, we’re introducing dynamic spot pricing for PVMs: an algorithm that reads available capacity and real-time demand signals from Nebius users to set the price automatically.

We launched preemptible VMs last year and they’ve become popular among our customers. Customers use PVMs to complete non-critical tasks like data processing, experimentation, and short fine-tuning jobs, significantly cutting their GPU compute costs. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### About Nebius Group
