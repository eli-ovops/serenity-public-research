## Social Responsibility Overview

Our people around the globe are the strength of our innovation, deriving from our unique cultural, geographic, racial and ethnic backgrounds. VPG supports human rights and carefully manages its human capital for its employees, third party contractors and suppliers.

What Ties Us Together- Our People

## Human Rights

VPG respects all internationally recognized human rights. We are committed to upholding fundamental human rights and believe all human beings around the world should be treated with dignity, fairness and respect, which extends to respecting and protecting the rights of minority groups and women.

## Ethics

Maintaining ethical business standards and practices is the highest priority at VPG. Our firm commitment to ethical business standards and practices guide all activities by VPG personnel, both internally and in interactions with VPG’s customers, suppliers, and subcontractors. VPG encourages and relies upon the integrity of all parties, both inside and outside the company, to maintain these ethical standards and practices.

Code of Business Conduct and Ethics

## ANTI-BRIBERY AND ANTI-CORRUPTION

As a part of VPG’s fundamental commitment to ethical business practices as described in VPG”s Global Code of Business Conduct, the VPG Board of Directors approved VPG’s global Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy. The policy applies to all VPG directors, officers, employees and to all agents and third party contractors who work on VPG’s behalf.

VPG requires all those covered by the policy to comply with both the letter and the spirit of the policy and of all applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws, including the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and the U.K. Bribery Act.

Anti-bribery and Anti-corruption Policy

Forming the foundation of this program is VPG’s identification of health and safety aspects and serious injury, fatality risks which form the basis of our operational controls thorough procedures, checklists and training.

VPG monitors the exposures of all VPG workers world-wide that may be exposed to hazardous substances or physical agents (noise, radiation, and temperature) annually as a part of each site’s industrial hygiene program. ## Employee Diversity And Inclusion, Demographics and Statistics

There is one consistent thread that ties everything together and ensures our continued success: Our People.

VPG is committed to providing an inclusive environment that respects diversity and reflects “Our Commitment to Each Other” outlined in our Global Code of Business Conduct. Our people around the globe are what ties us together and are the foundation of our success.  We are committed to the principles of equal employment opportunity, inclusion and respect.

VPG actively recruits people from diverse geographic, ethnic, and social backgrounds.  We believe new ideas from diverse perspectives lead to better business results. 

VPG seeks to provide employees a safe, productive, and collaborative work environment which values success and innovation, and which allows team members to fulfill their personal and professional potential.

We are proud of our globally diverse workforce that represents a powerful mix of talents and innovation. We strive to provide an innovative, diverse and rewarding environment which allows team members to fulfill their personal and professional potential and a wide variety of education and experience- based disciplines.
