# HPE teams with NVIDIA to advance secure agentic AI across enterprises and industrial environments

October 21, 2026

## HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA brings together accelerated infrastructure, governed AI services, and secure operations to help customers deploy and scale agentic AI across hybrid environments

In this article

  * HPE Private Cloud AI, the turnkey AI factory solution, expands agentic AI capabilities to provide developers, applications, and AI agents with secure access, governed AI agent integration, and greater GPU flexibility to help organizations move AI applications into production
  * New infrastructure innovations for AI factories include NVIDIA Vera CPU Rack by HPE, support for secure agent architectures and modular infrastructure to help organizations scale increasingly complex AI workloads
  * Expanded security and operational capabilities help enterprises and sovereign AI providers protect sensitive workloads, maintain control over infrastructure, and improve AI factory efficiency
  * HPE will support the NVIDIA OpenShell Reference Architecture for Safe Agents, delivering full-stack AI factory governance, isolation, and security with NVIDIA Vera Rubin, NVIDIA Vera CPUs, and NVIDIA BlueField-4

BERLIN – NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026 – Oct. 21, 2026 – HPE (NYSE: HPE) today announced new innovations across the HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA portfolio that help enterprises securely deploy and operate increasingly autonomous AI across hybrid and sovereign environments. HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA brings together models, infrastructure, data, and workflows to help enterprises run AI with greater control over performance and data access.

We’re helping customers scale AI across hybrid environments while maintaining the governance and operational control required to move from experimentation to production.

Cheri Williams

SVP & GM, Private Cloud & Flex Solutions, HPE

“AI agents are becoming an essential part of how enterprises operate and create value,” said Cheri Williams, SVP & GM, Private Cloud & Flex Solutions, HPE. “As organizations deploy AI across industries and increasingly sensitive environments, they need infrastructure that gives them greater control over security, sovereignty, and how enterprise data is accessed and used. We’re helping customers scale AI across hybrid environments while maintaining the governance and operational control required to move from experimentation to production.”
HPE Private Cloud AI expands secure access to enterprise AI services and agentic capabilities
For enterprises seeking to securely develop and scale AI applications, HPE is expanding HPE Private Cloud AI, the turnkey AI factory solution within the HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA portfolio, with new HPE AI Essentials capabilities that make it easier for developers, independent software vendors (ISVs), and AI agents to securely access, integrate and consume enterprise AI services. Through production-grade APIs and MCP servers, organizations can connect applications and autonomous agents to inference, model, and knowledge services while maintaining centralized governance, identity management, and operational control.
New capabilities include:

  * Governed APIs for enterprise AI: Developers and ISVs can programmatically and securely consume inference, models, knowledge services, and AI pipelines to integrate HPE Private Cloud AI into applications and industry solutions.
  * First-class MCP services for agentic AI: MCP servers are versioned and governed by role, tenant, and project, with auditing designed to enable AI agents to interact with enterprise systems while maintaining organizational governance.
  * OpenAPI-based development: OpenAPI serves as the source of truth for supported interfaces and enables automatically generated documentation, software development kits and code samples that help partners build integrations faster.
  * Enterprise-grade identity and governance: Role-based access control, machine identity support, and comprehensive audit logging are designed to help organizations manage human and autonomous access to AI services.
  * Support for regulated and sovereign environments: API-first and Kubernetes-assisted onboarding are now supported across both connected and air-gapped HPE Private Cloud AI deployments.

HPE is also expanding GPU flexibility within HPE Private Cloud AI to help enterprises efficiently support diverse AI workloads. Now generally available, HPE Private Cloud AI can combine NVIDIA H200 GPUs and NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs within the same cluster, enabling organizations to support a broader mix of AI workloads through a common, governed operating environment. NVIDIA’s open-source KAI Scheduler provides quota- and priority-based scheduling to improve GPU utilization and workload placement across projects.
Schneider Electric advances agentic industrial AI with HPE Private Cloud AI
Schneider Electric plans to combine HPE Private Cloud AI, co-engineered with NVIDIA, with its industrial and operational technology expertise to support Industrial AI Agents at the Edge, bringing intelligence closer to where operational decisions are made while maintaining control over sensitive data.
HPE and NVIDIA deliver purpose-built infrastructure for deploying and securing AI factories and agents
As organizations scale AI beyond individual applications, they require infrastructure that can efficiently support autonomous agents, secure execution environments and increasingly complex computing demands. HPE is expanding its AI infrastructure portfolio with NVIDIA technologies designed to help organizations build and deploy AI factories at scale.
New infrastructure innovations include:

  * Dedicated compute for agentic AI: HPE is introducing the NVIDIA Vera CPU Rack by HPE, a high-density, liquid-cooled system for CPU-intensive agentic AI and reinforcement learning (RL) workloads. With 256 NVIDIA Vera CPUs, support for 22.5K sandboxes, and 80% faster agentic CPU performance, it helps AI factories scale agent sandboxes, RL evaluations, and data-processing pipelines alongside GPU compute.
  * Secure execution for autonomous agents: HPE will support the NVIDIA OpenShell Reference Architecture for Safe AI Agents, an open, full-stack architecture for building, containing, observing and securing AI agents across AI factories, enterprise infrastructure, and the edge. The architecture combines the NVIDIA OpenShell secure runtime on NVIDIA Vera, NVIDIA Sentry with NVIDIA BlueField-4 for hardware-based security enforcement, and NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 for model inference, helping enterprises govern agent activity, isolate workloads and enforce security policies as agentic AI deployments scale. HPE and NVIDIA are collaborating to extend security and protection from the agent runtime to the underlying AI infrastructure throughout the HPE AI Factory with NVIDIA portfolio.
  * Simplified AI factory deployment: HPE will also support NVIDIA DSX POD, a reference design for pre-fabricated and validated infrastructure in modular containers to reduce onsite integration work and shorten deployment timelines for large-scale AI factory environments. HPE will work with NVIDIA to deliver DSX POD AI factories, building upon the modular data center expertise and services offered today with AI Mod Pod. 

HPE strengthen security and operations for sovereign AI infrastructure with NVIDIA
Organizations deploying AI in sovereign and regulated environments need infrastructure that protects sensitive data and models while maintaining control over security, performance and operations.
HPE is expanding support for NVIDIA technologies that address these requirements across AI factory environments:

  * Confidential computing for sensitive AI workloads: To help address security and sovereignty requirements, HPE is expanding support for NVIDIA Confidential Computing on HPE ProLiant Compute DL380a Gen12 servers equipped with NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs, helping organizations secure frontier AI models and protect sensitive data on sovereign infrastructure while workloads are in use. On NVIDIA-Certified Servers, confidential computing establishes hardware-based trusted execution environments designed to isolate AI workloads, including virtual machines, containers, and bare metal deployments, from underlying infrastructure.

Availability

  * Updates to HPE Private Cloud AI with the HPE AI Essentials API platform, including administrator-enabled developer and ISV access, are rolling out in late 2026.
  * Included in HPE Private Cloud AI is a self-service HPE AI Essentials developer experience for application and agent registration, API and MCP discovery, and credential management. This will be rolling out in 2027.
  * Heterogeneous GPU support for NVIDIA H200 and NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition GPUs within HPE Private Cloud AI is generally available now.
  * KAI Scheduler capabilities for HPE Private Cloud AI are generally available now.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HPE secures its first AMD Helios order in $1.2 billion deal with Vultr

Vultr will deploy first AMD Helios™ AI Rack by HPE systems featuring new purpose-built HPE Networking scale-up switching at its U.S. data centers

SUNNYVALE, Calif. – Sept. 30, 2026 – HPE (NYSE: HPE) today announced a $1.2 billion order from Vultr, the world’s largest privately-held cloud infrastructure company, to deploy AMD Helios™ AI Rack by HPE systems at Vultr’s cloud data center locations in the United States. Record results and demand drive higher outlook for fiscal 2026 and fiscal 2027

HOUSTON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--HPE (NYSE: HPE) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended July 31, 2026.

“HPE’s strategy is proving itself again this quarter. Our results demonstrate the durability of our profitable growth momentum. We delivered record revenue, orders, and profitability, fueled by surging customer demand across our portfolio,” said Antonio Neri, president and CEO of HPE. “AI is becoming a multi-year growth driver for HPE, and our differentiated portfolio positions us to capture that opportunity at scale.”
